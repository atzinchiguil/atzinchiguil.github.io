Francisco Atzin Chiguil Rojas

Ph.D. Student in Economics · Rice University

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Economics at Rice University. My research interests lie in macroeconomics, monetary economics, labor economics, and macroeconomic policy.

Before joining the Ph.D. program at Rice University, I worked as an economist at Banco de México in the Governing Board, as an analyst at the Ministry of Finance, and as a research assistant at El Colegio de México.

I hold a B.A. in Economics from the National Autonomous University of Mexico and an M.A. in Economics from El Colegio de México.

Research interests: Macroeconomics, Monetary Economics, Labor Economics.
